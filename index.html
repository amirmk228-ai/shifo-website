About Shifo

PO “Shifo” is a Tajik public organization based in Shahrituz, Khatlon, registered with the Ministry of Justice since 2013. We have worked with IOM since 2008 and have delivered projects for the World Bank, GIZ, IOM, the EU and Caritas Germany. Our mission is to raise legal, social, economic and environmental literacy and to support enterprise that reduces rural poverty.

Smallholder farmers in the basin face heat, water scarcity, erosion and salty soils, and structured technical advice has rarely reached them. We turn the informal network of local agronomists and lead farmers into a trained, recognized extension service linked to Jamoat Water Action Plans.

Our three-tier approach

Knowledge moves down three tiers, and each tier teaches the next.

Tier one: the expert team

Agronomists and practice specialists design each demonstration plot together with the lead farmer and teach the methods on site, by doing.

Tier two: the lead farmer

Owns the plot, completes training of trainers, and becomes the extension provider for the jamoat. Payment is per training round delivered.

Tier three: the farmers of the jamoat

Learn at the plot through seasonal Farmer Field School rounds, then ask their lead farmer for advice between rounds.

Plots belong to farmers, not to the project, so the learning continues after the project ends.

Where we work: 14 jamoats in 6 districts

Select a jamoat to see its farming profile. The river runs from the northern mountains to the southern lowlands.

Farming profiles are preliminary and are confirmed with each jamoat during the project’s inception phase.
